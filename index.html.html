Course Profiles

Know before you go — history, what to expect, and tips for each track.

County Louth (Baltray)
Links
📍 Baltray, Co. Louth · Sep 12
6,936Yards
73Par
Est. 1892Founded
Known simply as "Baltray," one of Ireland's most beloved hidden gems — set on the Boyne Estuary with classic duneland terrain, superb greens, and almost no crowds. A perfect warm-up opener.
DunelandBoyne EstuaryHidden gem
Know Before You Go

The par-5 17th is a brute. Wind off the estuary can shift mid-round. Greens are fast and true.

Corballis
Public Links
📍 Donabate, Co. Dublin · Sep 13 (Option A — Early AM)
5,800Yards
71Par
PublicAccess
A wonderful public links on the Fingal coast. Natural dunes with Lambay Island views. A relaxed, affordable early-morning stretch before the main event at Portmarnock. Great scenery, no pressure.
Public courseLambay viewsBudget gemRelaxed start
Know Before You Go

Keep it low-key — big afternoon at Portmarnock ahead. Don't overthink it. Enjoy the dunes and get the rust out of the swing.

Jameson Links (Portmarnock Resort)
Championship Links
📍 Portmarnock, Co. Dublin · Sep 13 (Option B — Early AM)
7,466Yards
72Par
Est. 1995Founded
Designed by Bernhard Langer on the historic Jameson Whiskey estate, right next door to Portmarnock GC. Extensively redesigned in 2023 by architect Jeff Lynch. Ranked #26 in Ireland (Irish Golfer 2025) — elevated tees, massive dunes, 98 bunkers, and sharp doglegs. A real step up from Corballis and a genuinely strong morning round before Portmarnock in the afternoon.
Bernhard Langer designRedesigned 2023#26 in IrelandJameson EstateChampionship links
Know Before You Go

Effectively two top-tier rounds in one day — legs and scorecard will feel it. The 12th hole has a tabletop green with incredible views over the Velvet Strand, Howth Head, and Lambay Island. A pint in the historic Jameson Bar afterwards is non-negotiable.

Portmarnock Golf Club
Championship Links
📍 Portmarnock, Co. Dublin · Sep 13
7,366Yards
72Par
Est. 1894Founded
Consistently ranked in the world's top 30 and a multiple Irish Open host. A narrow peninsula with wind from every direction — no two rounds alike.
Top 30 WorldIrish Open hostPure links
Know Before You Go

Wind dictates everything. Club up on approaches. The par-3 15th is one of the finest holes in Ireland.

Royal County Down
World Top 5
📍 Newcastle, Co. Down · Sep 14
7,186Yards
71Par
Est. 1889Founded
Ranked top 3–5 on Earth. Beneath the Mourne Mountains with Dundrum Bay as backdrop. Blind shots, heather, gorse — visually breathtaking and brutally challenging.
World Top 5Mourne MountainsBlind shots
Know Before You Go

Get a caddie — non-negotiable. The 9th tee shot is arguably the most dramatic view in golf. Walk slowly. Soak every second in.

RCD Annesley Links
Links
📍 Newcastle, Co. Down · Sep 14 (PM)
4,681Yards
65Par
CompanionCourse
The companion course at Royal County Down — shorter but delightful. Perfect afternoon complement after the main event.
Afternoon roundCompanion course
Know Before You Go

Keep it fun and relaxed. Perfect format for settling morning grudges from Championship RCD.

Royal Portrush — Dunluce
The Open Venue
📍 Portrush, Co. Antrim · Sep 15
7,344Yards
72Par
Est. 1888Founded
Open Championship host — most recently 2019. A muscular, dramatic links on the Causeway Coast clifftops. The Atlantic views are breathtaking. Calamity Corner awaits.
Open ChampionshipWorld Top 10Causeway Coast
Know Before You Go

Hole 5 "White Rocks" drops to the Atlantic — take the photo. Par-3 14th "Calamity" is brutal; respect the left edge.

Portstewart Strand
Championship Links
📍 Portstewart, Co. Derry · Sep 16
7,057Yards
72Par
Est. 1894Founded
Ranked in the world's top 75 — the Strand opens with what many consider the finest opening hole in links golf: a descent through towering dunes to the Atlantic shore.
Top 75 WorldIrish Open hostBest opening hole
Know Before You Go

If you're not fired up after the first tee shot, check your pulse. The opening three holes through the dunes are unmissable.

Castlerock Golf Club
Links
📍 Castlerock, Co. Derry · Sep 16 (PM)
6,656Yards
73Par
Est. 1901Founded
Between the River Bann and the sea — rugged, natural links. Not as famous as its neighbours but utterly charming. Perfect afternoon cap to the two-course Causeway day.
River BannRugged links
Know Before You Go

Mussenden Temple ruins on the cliffs above the back nine add an otherworldly backdrop.

Ballyliffin — Glashedy
Championship Links
📍 Inishowen Peninsula, Co. Donegal · Sep 17
7,165Yards
72Par
Irish Open 2018Host
Ireland's most northerly club on the wild Inishowen Peninsula. Remote, dramatic, and breathtaking. Wind-lashed duneland at the very edge of Europe.
Irish Open 2018Wild & remoteIreland's north
Know Before You Go

The par-4 15th with Glashedy Rock in the background is a photo you will frame and hang on your wall.

Rosapenna — Sandy Hills
Tom Doak Design
📍 Downings, Co. Donegal · Sep 18 (PM)
6,993Yards
71Par
Tom DoakArchitect
Tom Doak's celebrated modern masterpiece — a wild ride through massive dunes above Sheephaven Bay. One of Ireland's best-kept secrets.
Tom Doak designSheephaven BayModern masterpiece
Know Before You Go

The dunes are enormous — stay below the hole on approaches. Sandy Hills rewards local knowledge.

Cruit Island
9-Hole Links
📍 Kincasslagh, Co. Donegal · Sep 19
5,035Yards
68Par
9 holesLayout
A tiny gem on a tidal island off the Donegal coast. Nine holes played twice — pure, raw, unspoiled golf with Atlantic views in every direction.
Tidal islandRaw & pureAtlantic views
Know Before You Go

Hole 4 tee shot over the Atlantic is one of the most scenic in Ireland. Do not rush this one.
